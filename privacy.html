Keep & Claw — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. Made by Banx Studios.

The short version

Keep & Claw does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. We never see who you are or how you play.

Your saved game

Your progress is saved only on your own device (in the app's storage, or your browser's storage when you play on the web). It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data deletes it.

If you have switched on Google's backup on your Android phone, Android may include the game's save in that backup, so a new phone or a reinstall can bring it back. That backup is between you and Google; we cannot see it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

The game's Settings also let you copy your save as a line of text, so you can move it to another device yourself. That text stays wherever you paste it; we never see it.

Purchases

The game has optional in-app purchases (for example Gems, the Starter Pack or Double Gold). They are handled entirely by Google Play: we never see your name, card or payment details. Google tells the app only that a purchase went through, and the app keeps a note of it on your device (the order number and what was bought) so nothing is ever given twice and for-good purchases come back after a reinstall. Google's own privacy policy covers the payment itself.

Nothing random is ever sold for real money, and every hero and every event can be reached for free.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app can show at most one reminder a day (for example "Today: Beast Hunt!" or when an event is about to end). These are made and shown by your phone itself; nothing is sent over the internet. Each kind can be switched off in Settings, or all of them in your phone's settings.

Internet

The phone app does not need the internet to play: everything it uses is inside the app. Only buying something in the Shop needs a connection, to reach Google Play. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version ever adds something that collects data (for example crash reports or a cloud save), this page will be updated first and the change will be listed here.

Contact

Questions: use the developer email on Keep & Claw's Google Play page.
