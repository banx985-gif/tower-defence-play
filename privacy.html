Keep & Claw — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026. Made by Banx Games.

The short version

Keep & Claw does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your saved game

Your progress is saved only on your own device (in the app's storage, or your browser's storage when you play on the web). It is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data deletes it.

The game's Settings let you copy your save as a line of text, so you can move it to another device yourself. That text stays wherever you paste it; we never see it.

Internet

The phone app does not need the internet to play: everything it uses is inside the app. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version ever adds something that collects data (for example crash reports), this page will be updated first and the change will be listed here.

Contact

Questions: use the developer email on Keep & Claw's Google Play page.
