Keep & Claw: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026. Keep & Claw is made by Banx Studios (Aaron Banks).

The short version

Keep & Claw does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. We never see who you are or how you play, and we sell nothing about you to anyone.

Your saved game

Your progress is saved only on your own device, in the app's own storage (or your browser's storage if you play on the web). The game also keeps up to three automatic copies of your save on the device, so you can go back if something goes wrong. None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data deletes it.

If you have Google backup switched on for your Android phone, Android may include the game's save in that backup, so a new phone or a reinstall can bring it back. That backup is between you and Google; we cannot see it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Settings also lets you copy your save as a line of text, to move it to another device yourself. That text only goes where you paste it.

Error log

If the game hits an error, it writes a short note in a small file on your device: the time, the game's version, which screen was open and the technical error message. It holds no personal information. It is never sent anywhere automatically. You can copy it from Settings (Copy error log) and send it to us yourself if you want help, and you can clear it there too.

Purchases

The game has optional in-app purchases (for example Gems, the Starter Pack or Double Gold). They are handled entirely by Google Play: we never see your name, card or payment details. Google tells the app only that a purchase went through, and the app keeps a note of it on your device (the order number and what was bought), so it can be restored. Google's own privacy policy applies to the payment.

Nothing random is ever sold for real money, and every hero and every event can be reached for free.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app can show at most one reminder a day (for example "Today: Beast Hunt!" or when an event is about to end). These are made and shown by your phone itself; nothing is sent over the internet. Each kind can be switched off in Settings, or all of them in your phone's settings.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, the game can keep a copy of your save in your own Google Play Games account (so your progress can follow you to a new phone), unlock achievements and post your best scores to leaderboards (Beast Tower floor, Grand Siege and Daily Trial). This is handled by Google Play Games under your Google account and Google's privacy policy; we do not receive your name, email or the save. You never need to sign in to play, and you can sign out in the Google Play Games app.

Internet

The phone app does not need the internet to play: everything it uses is inside the app. Only buying something in the Shop, and Google Play Games if you sign in, need a connection. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your choices

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything from your device, uninstall the app.

Changes

If a future version ever adds something that collects data (for example online crash reports or a cloud save), this page will be updated first and the change will be listed here.

Contact

Questions: use the developer email on Keep & Claw's Google Play page.
